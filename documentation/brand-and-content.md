# Brand & Content Brief — snapdesign

> For content writers. This explains **what snapdesign is**, who it is for, how it should sound,
> and what every page, section and card needs to say. It does not explain how the product works
> internally, and you don't need that to write it.

_Last updated: 2026-09-26_

---

## 1. What we are building

**snapdesign turns a written description into a finished, professional-looking design in seconds.**

Someone types what they need in plain words, for example "a landing page for my roofing company"
or "a launch poster for a coffee brand", and gets back a polished, high-quality design they can
download and use straight away.

It's for people who need good design fast and don't want to open design software, brief an
agency, or settle for a generic template.

### What a person gets
- **Website designs**: landing pages, homepages, hero sections
- **App screens**: mobile and web app UI
- **Marketing visuals**: social posts, ads, campaign graphics
- **Graphic design**: posters, brand visuals, illustrations
- A result they can **refine by chatting** ("make it darker", "more minimal", "try another
  version") and **download** as a high-resolution image
- **Full ownership.** Everything they make is theirs to use commercially.

### The one-line version
> Describe it. snapdesign designs it.

## 3. What makes us different

Use these as the pillars of all copy. Every section should land at least one.

1. **Real design, not AI slop.** Our designs look like a good designer made them: considered
   typography, real layouts, proper spacing. No purple gradients or generic stock-AI look. This
   is our biggest differentiator. Lean on it.
2. **Seconds, not weeks.** A finished design, usually on the first try.
3. **No skills needed.** Plain language in, professional design out.
4. **Yours to keep.** Commercial use included on every plan.

## 4. Voice & tone

**We sound like:** a sharp, friendly design lead. Confident, warm, a little witty. Never
salesy, never techy.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short, punchy sentences | Long feature-list paragraphs |
| Talk about the result ("a landing page you'd be proud of") | Talk about the technology ("our advanced AI model…") |
| Concrete examples (roofing company, coffee brand, fitness app) | Vague claims ("unlock limitless creativity") |
| Confident, plain words | Hype ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "10x") |
| "you" and "your" | "users", "leverage", "solutions" |

### Words we use
design · describe · seconds · polished · on-brand · first try · yours · real design

### Words we avoid
AI-powered (don't lead with it) · revolutionary · seamless · unleash · supercharge · leverage ·
cutting-edge · magic (sparingly, at most once per page)

### Name & style rules
- The brand is **snapdesign**, always lowercase, one word, even at the start of a sentence.
- The domain is **snapdesign.ai**. Use it only when referring to the website itself.
- Never "Snap Design", "SnapDesign" or "Snapdesign".
- Sentence case for all headings and buttons ("Start designing", not "Start Designing").

## 5. ⚠️ Claims guardrail: say only what's true

The product creates **finished design images**. Don't promise things it doesn't do. Check this
list before publishing any line.

| ✅ Safe to say | ❌ Do not say (not true / not built) |
|---|---|
| Generates website, app, marketing and graphic designs | "Editable output, not a flat image" (it **is** an image) |
| Refine by chatting / ask for variations | "Exports to Figma / code / HTML" |
| Download in high resolution | "Publish your website" / "Get a live site" |
| Commercial use included | "Unlimited" anything (plans are credit-based) |
| Works in seconds | Specific speed numbers ("in 3 seconds") unless confirmed |
| Credit-based plans with a free trial | Brand kits, team workspaces, API, affiliate programme (**not built yet**, see §7) |

If a line needs a feature marked "not built", flag it to the product owner instead of writing it.

## 6. Page-by-page content brief

Each block lists: **Purpose** · **Must convey** · **Current copy** · **Action** · **Length**.

### 6.1 Browser tab & search result (site metadata)
- **Purpose:** what shows in Google and on the browser tab.
- **Current:** title "snapdesign.ai" · description "AI design generation — describe it, get it."
- **Action:** rewrite both.
- **Length:** title ≤ 60 characters · description 140–160 characters.
- **Direction:** name + what it does. e.g. *"snapdesign: turn a description into a finished design"*.

### 6.2 Navigation bar
- **Current links:** Mockups (dropdown) · How it works · About · Sign in · **Design** (main button)
- **Action:** confirm link labels still fit. Main button "Design" can stay, or become
  "Start designing" (max 2 words, it's a small pill).
- **Length:** each link 1–2 words.

### 6.3 Hero (first thing anyone sees) · HIGHEST PRIORITY
- **Purpose:** in three seconds, a visitor must know *what this is* and *why it's better*.
- **Current headline:** "**Outbound** agents for every rep". ❌ **Wrong product, leftover
  placeholder. Must be replaced.**
- **Layout rule:** the headline is a two-part lockup. The **first word** is set extra large and
  bold, and the rest of the line sits beside it in a lighter weight. Write it so the first word can
  carry the weight alone.
- **Length:** 3–7 words total. First word ideally one strong word.
- **Must convey:** describe → get a real design. Hint at quality (not slop).
- **Directions to explore:**
  - *"**Describe** it. We'll design it."*
  - *"**Design** anything in a sentence"*
  - *"**Real** design from a single sentence"*

### 6.4 Prompt box placeholder (the grey hint text inside the input)
- **Purpose:** show people what to type. It's the most-read "copy" on the page.
- **Current:** "Find 10 top-rated catering companies in San Francisco and their owners". ❌
  **Wrong product. Must be replaced.**
- **Length:** 50–90 characters. One realistic, specific request.
- **Direction:** a concrete design request, ideally for a relatable small business.
  e.g. *"A bold landing page for a family-run roofing company in Austin"*.
- Optional: supply 4–6 of these so the product can rotate them.

### 6.5 Docked prompt bar (appears at the bottom as you scroll)
- Uses the **same placeholder** as the hero box. No separate copy needed.

### 6.6 "How it works" section (four cards)
- **Current heading:** "How it works"
- ⚠️ **Mismatch to fix:** the heading promises an explanation, but the four cards are
  *audiences* (Creators, Studios, Affiliates, Partners). Pick one:
  - **A (recommended):** rename the heading to something like *"Made for how you work"* and
    keep audience cards, or
  - **B:** keep "How it works" and rewrite the cards as **steps** (Describe → Refine → Download
    → Use it anywhere).
- **Per card:** badge (2–4 words) · title (1–2 words) · body (90–130 characters) · link
  (2–3 words).

| Card | Current body | Status | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Creators** · "Open / Free to start" | "Generate mockups, templates and full design sets from a single prompt, across every format you ship." | 🟡 Soften | "Full design sets" and "every format" overpromise. Focus: posts, ads, visuals in seconds. |
| **Studios** · "Open for teams" | "Run client work at speed with shared brand kits, unlimited exports and a workspace per project." | ❌ Rewrite | Brand kits, unlimited exports and workspaces are **not built**. Focus: explore directions fast for pitches. |
| **Affiliates** · "Invite only" | "Refer studios and creators to snapdesign and earn recurring revenue on everything they generate." | ❌ Hold | No affiliate programme exists. Replace the card (e.g. **Founders**) or confirm the programme first. |
| **Partners** · "Invite only" | "Offer your customers direct access to snapdesign through our API, under your own brand." | ❌ Hold | No API exists. Replace (e.g. **Developers**) or confirm first. |

Suggested replacement set (matches §2): **Founders · Creators · Studios · Developers.**

### 6.7 FAQ (five questions)
- **Heading:** eyebrow "FAQ" · title "Frequently Asked Questions" → change to sentence case:
  "Frequently asked questions".
- **Answer length:** 1–3 sentences, ≤ 300 characters.

| Question | Status | Note for the writer |
|---|---|---|
| What can I generate with snapdesign? | ❌ Fix | Remove "returns editable output, not a flat image". Say: website, app, marketing and graphic designs, delivered as high-res images you can refine and download. |
| Do I need design experience to use it? | 🟡 Fix | Keep "No, plain language". Remove "every result opens in the editor so you can take over". Replace with "refine it by chatting". |
| Who owns the designs I generate? | ✅ Keep | True. "on every plan including the free tier" is fine if a free tier exists at launch. Confirm. |
| Can I use my own brand kit? | ❌ Hold | Brand kits are not built. Replace with a true question, e.g. *"Can I ask for changes?"* or *"What makes it different from other AI tools?"* |
| How does pricing work? | 🟡 Fix | Credits = true. Remove "unlimited exports and shared workspaces". |

### 6.8 Footer
- **Newsletter line (current):** "Sign up for our curated edit of new mockups & templates". 🟡
  Fine in tone. Update if we're not sending "templates". e.g. *"The best designs made with
  snapdesign, in your inbox."* (≤ 70 characters).
- **Link columns:** Browse (Home, Mockups, Templates, Showcase, Limited Edition, Free Stuff) ·
  About (How it works, Pricing, Help Centre, Contact) · Legal. "Limited Edition" and "Free
  Stuff" have no meaning in this product. Rename or remove. Keep labels 1–2 words.
- **Spelling:** pick US *or* UK English and apply everywhere ("Help Centre" vs "colour" vs
  "Center"). Recommended: **US English**.

### 6.9 Sign up page (left promo panel + form)
- **Panel kicker (current):** "You can easily" 🟡 incomplete-sounding.
- **Panel headline (current):** "Get access to your personal hub for clarity and productivity."
  ❌ Generic, sounds like a notes app. Rewrite around designing.
- **Form title/blurb:** "Create an account" / "Generate mockups, templates and full design sets
  from a single prompt — and keep every project in one place." 🟡 Tighten, drop "full design sets".
- **Length:** kicker 2–5 words · headline ≤ 70 characters · blurb ≤ 120 characters.

### 6.10 Log in page
- **Kicker:** "Good to see you again" ✅ · **Headline:** "Pick up right where you left off and
  keep designing." ✅ · **Blurb:** "Sign in to pick up your projects right where you left off." 🟡
  repeats the headline. Vary it.

### 6.11 404 page
- **Heading:** "This page went in the bin." ✅ on-voice, witty. Keep.
- **Body:** "The page you're looking for doesn't exist, or it has moved. Let's get you back to
  something useful." ✅ Keep.
